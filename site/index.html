GuardianLink Surface

A record that keeps itself straight.

GuardianLink Surface is a small app for keeping track of what's true right now — and noticing when it stops agreeing with itself. You add what happened, in plain words, and ask what it means. If two entries contradict each other, Surface shows you the conflict instead of quietly picking a winner. If you correct something, the correction stays tied to the original, out in the open.

That's the GuardianLink idea in miniature: you hand your cares over, and GuardianLink holds them — keeps them straight, watches them on your behalf, and acts in your interest by default. The only thing that ever overrides it is you, making a deliberate decision of your own. Then it steps aside, and your call goes on the record like everything else. This demo is the record-keeping piece of that idea — the part you can touch today.

No account. No cloud. Nothing leaves your device. It runs entirely in your browser or on your phone, and it forgets everything when you close it — unless you save a copy yourself. Smooth on the surface; underneath, every answer is worked out the same careful way, every time.
